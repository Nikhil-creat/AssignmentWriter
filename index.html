Questions, answered.

Is it really free?+

The site is free. You use your own free Groq or Gemini key, which have their own usage limits.

Where do I get a key?+

Groq keys start with gsk_ and come from console.groq.com/keys. Gemini keys start with AIza and come from aistudio.google.com/apikey.

Can I trust the answers?+

Treat them as a strong draft. AI can make mistakes, so check facts, formulas and code against your textbook.

Can I submit AI-written work?+

Check your college's rules on AI-assisted work first. Sheetstudio is best used to learn, revise and prepare.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes. The studio has Edit and Preview tabs on small screens, and PDF export uses your browser's print dialog.

Does it work offline?+

After your first visit the studio opens offline and you can edit, preview and print. Generating answers needs internet. On Android, use your browser menu to install it as an app.

My key is rejected. What now?+

Create a fresh key, paste it without spaces, and tap Verify and save. The studio tells you right away if it is invalid.
